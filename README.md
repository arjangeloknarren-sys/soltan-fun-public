# Soltan.fun

### Explore. Create. Build on Solana.

Soltan.fun is a platform focused on token creation, market discovery, liquidity tools, and community features within the Solana ecosystem.

Our goal is to make it easier for creators and enthusiasts to explore projects, launch tokens, access supported liquidity features, and discover communities in one place.

## What Soltan.fun Offers

- **Token Launching** — Tools for creating tokens on Solana.
- **Token Explorer** — Explore tokens and available market data.
- **Liquidity Tools** — Access supported liquidity pool features.
- **Community Features** — Discover projects and connect with communities.
- **Ongoing Development** — Continuous improvements guided by our long-term vision.

## Our Vision

We believe strong ecosystems are built through transparency, consistent development, and community participation. Soltan.fun is being developed step by step, with the ambition to create a more accessible and connected Solana ecosystem.
